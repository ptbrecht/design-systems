# Local Energy — localenergy.com

Website system for Local Energy. **Not** Falcon West, Falcon West Energy, or Material. Do not mix tokens, type, or buttons with those folders.

Tan `#D49A45` is the brand field. The page is paper `#F2F2F2`. Red `#BA0C2F` is the accent and the primary button, with a paper label. `#0B1215` is the only dark surface. `#191919` is ink on light grounds, not a panel. The star is the California and Texas state star: a solid upright pentagram, no stroke, and nothing else from either flag.

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) | Spec. Literal values, contrast ratios, binding rules. |
| [tokens.json](tokens.json) | The same roles as data. |
| [globals.css](globals.css) | Drop-in Tailwind v4 theme for the Next.js site. |
| [VOICE.md](VOICE.md) | Headlines and blog copy. Applies [`../writing/voice.md`](../writing/voice.md). |
| [specimen.html](specimen.html) / [specimen.png](specimen.png) | Visual specimen. The PNG is a render of the HTML. |

Marks, favicon, and the social card live in [`../assets/logos/local-energy/`](../assets/logos/local-energy/). Archivo Black, Archivo, and Lora live in [`../fonts/`](../fonts/) under the OFL. They are not the Falcon West faces. Archivo Black is display, h1, and h2. Archivo at 700 and 800 is the smaller headings and the chrome. Lora is body.

**Recommendation, short form:** this is a website system, like falconwest.com. It is not a Material theme. A future app, if one is built, would be a Material face — see DESIGN.md §10.
